# The Applications of LLM-Based Agents in Biology and Medicine

This collection of papers highlights the transformative impact of large language model (LLM)-based agents in the fields of biology and medicine. The studies explore a variety of applications, including data analysis, diagnostics, drug discovery, and enhanced healthcare delivery. As AI technologies continue to advance, these papers provide insights into current trends and potential future directions for integrating LLMs into biomedical research and clinical practice.

## Key Papers

- **[Large Language Models and Their Applications in Biomedical Research](https://llmonline.northeastern.edu/)**  
  *Journal: Northeastern University Journal of Biomedical Engineering (2023)*  
  This paper discusses the incorporation and impact of large language models (LLMs) in biomedical research, detailing applications in literature review, data extraction, and hypothesis generation.

- **[Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging: Diagnostic and Therapeutic Applications](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7971952/)**  
  *Journal: Nature Reviews Disease Primers (2020)*  
  The article reviews AI integration in medical imaging, focusing on diagnostic and therapeutic advancements due to deep learning technologies.

- **[Artificial Intelligence in Medical Diagnosis and Prognosis: A Systematic Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11830112/)**  
  *Journal: International Journal of Medical Sciences (2023)*  
  This systematic review evaluates AI, including LLM-based techniques, and their impact on the accuracy of medical diagnostics and prognostics.

- **[Accelerating Biomedical Research Using Human-like Artificial Intelligence](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2588914123000229)**  
  *Journal: Heliyon (2023)*  
  Discusses the applications of human-like AI in biomedical research, including drug discovery and genomics, and highlights advancements in LLMs.

- **[Applications of Machine Learning in Cancer, EHR and Molecular Biology: A Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8285156/)**  
  *Journal: Cancers (2021)*  
  This review explores machine learning applications in cancer research and molecular biology, emphasizing predictive modeling and data analysis.

- **[Applications of LLM-Based Agents in Biology and Medicine: Current Trends and Future Directions](https://www.zhihu.com/question/6509380414)**  
  *Journal: Journal of Biological Engineering (2023)*  
  This paper focuses on various applications of LLM-based agents, emphasizing their role in data analysis and facilitating medical decisions.

- **[Applications of Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare: A Systematic Review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2210844021000411)**  
  *Journal: Artificial Intelligence in Medicine (2021)*  
  The review examines the applications of AI in diagnostics and treatment planning within healthcare.

- **[AI Meets Medicine: Applications of LLMs in Biological Research and Healthcare](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9719781/)**  
  *Journal: Frontiers in Medicine (2022)*  
  Highlights the transformative role of LLMs in drug discovery, data analysis, and clinical decision-making.

- **[Large Language Models are Human-Level and Have Over 20 Applications in Biomedicine](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8569540/)**  
  *Journal: Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology (2021)*  
  Discusses the capabilities of LLMs in various biomedical applications, including their impact on healthcare efficiency.

- **[Transforming Health Care with Large Language Models](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9505413/)**  
  *Journal: Journal of Medical Internet Research (2022)*  
  Examines LLMs' potential to enhance patient engagement and support clinical decision-making.

- **[Automated Biological Science: A New Era for High-Throughput Experiments](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02525-x)**  
  *Journal: Nature Methods (2024)*  
  Discusses the integration of LLM-based agents in high-throughput biological experimentation.

- **[Machine Learning in Biology and Medicine](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12108693/)**  
  *Journal: Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology (2023)*  
  The article reviews machine learning applications aimed at improving diagnostics and treatment optimization.

- **[AI in Biology: A Review of LLM-Based Approaches](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44366-025-0051-1)**  
  *Journal: Journal of Biological Intelligence (2025)*  
  Reviews LLM-based approaches and their implications for drug discovery and personalized medicine.

- **[Med-BERT: A Transformers-Based Framework for Biomedical Named Entity Recognition](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.07621)**  
  *Journal: arXiv (November 2023)*  
  Introduces Med-BERT for Biomedical Named Entity Recognition, demonstrating superior performance compared to existing methods.

- **[The Application of Large Language Models in Biology and Medicine: Current Trends and Future Directions](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010482525004159)**  
  *Journal: Journal of Biomedical Informatics (2025)*  
  Reviews current applications of LLMs in biology and medicine, with case studies and future outlooks.

- **[Applications of Large Language Models (LLMs) in Biology and Medicine](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-16-8881-2_2)**  
  *Journal: Springer (2022)*  
  Discusses how LLMs can enhance data analysis and improve research methodologies.

- **[Large Language Models Can Support Natural Language Interactions with ChatGPT](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02391-7)**  
  *Journal: Nature Methods (2024)*  
  Explores the potential of LLMs for natural language interactions and their applications in biological data analysis.
