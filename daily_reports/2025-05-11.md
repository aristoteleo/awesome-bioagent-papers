# The Applications of LLM-Based Agents in Biology and Medicine

This document compiles significant research exploring the integration and applications of large language models (LLMs) in biology and medicine. The studies highlighted here address various aspects, including data analysis, drug discovery, clinical decision-making, and patient care, emphasizing the transformative potential of LLMs in enhancing the efficiency and accuracy of biomedical research and healthcare.

## List of Papers
