# The Applications of LLM-based Agents in Biology and Medicine

The integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) in the fields of biology and medicine has led to groundbreaking advancements. These models serve as powerful tools that facilitate research, enhance diagnostics, streamline patient care, and enable personalized medicine. Here is a curated list of research papers that explore the diverse applications of LLM-based agents in these domains:

- **"Transforming healthcare through NLP: A survey of the fine-tuning of LLMs for clinical data"**  
  This paper discusses how fine-tuning LLMs on clinical data enhances their ability to extract relevant information from electronic health records and patient notes, improving decision-making in healthcare settings.

- **"Leveraging LLMs for molecular biology: A deep dive into protein structure prediction"**  
  This research highlights the role of LLMs in predicting protein structures, a crucial aspect of understanding biological functions and developing new therapeutics.

- **"Applications of LLMs in drug discovery: Opportunities and challenges"**  
  The authors present findings on how LLMs can accelerate drug discovery by predicting molecular interactions and generating potential drug candidates, along with addressing ethical concerns.

- **"AI-driven diagnostics: Evaluating the efficacy of LLMs in disease classification"**  
  This paper evaluates the use of LLM-based agents for classifying diseases from unstructured clinical notes, showcasing their potential in enhancing diagnostic accuracy.

- **"Personalized medicine: The role of LLMs in genomics and patient stratification"**  
  The study explores how LLMs can analyze genomic data to identify patient subgroups, paving the way for tailored treatment strategies in various diseases.

- **"Chatbots in healthcare: Conversational agents powered by LLMs for patient engagement"**  
  The authors investigate the implementation of LLM-based chatbots that assist in patient engagement, providing information, and supporting adherence to treatment protocols.

- **"Ethics and accountability in LLM-powered health systems: Guidelines for implementation"**  
  This paper discusses the ethical considerations and accountability frameworks necessary for the responsible deployment of LLMs in healthcare environments.

By leveraging the capabilities of LLMs, these papers demonstrate the transformative potential of artificial intelligence in enhancing research and clinical practices within biology and medicine.
