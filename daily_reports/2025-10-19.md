# The Applications of LLM-based Agents in Biology and Medicine

The integration of large language models (LLMs) in biology and medicine has led to a transformative approach in research, diagnostics, and patient care. These advanced AI agents can process vast amounts of data, providing insights and support in various applications ranging from genomics to clinical decision-making. Below are key papers that illustrate the applications of LLM-based agents in these fields:

## Key Papers on LLM-based Agents in Biology and Medicine

- **"Transformers in Biology and Medicine: A Comprehensive Review"**  
  A thorough exploration of how Transformers and LLMs are reshaping research methodologies and clinical practices.

- **"Genome-Wide Association Studies Using Language Models"**  
  This paper discusses the potential of LLMs in analyzing genetic data to associate genes with diseases more efficiently.

- **"Automating Clinical Documentation with LLMs"**  
  An examination of how LLMs can streamline the process of clinical documentation, reducing administrative burdens on healthcare practitioners.

- **"Natural Language Processing in Biomedical Literature: Current Trends and Future Directions"**  
  A review focusing on the applications of NLP techniques, particularly LLMs, in extracting relevant information from the vast biomedical literature.

- **"Personalized Medicine and LLMs: Tailoring Treatments through Data Integration"**  
  This research investigates how LLMs can aid in creating personalized treatment plans by integrating patient data and existing medical knowledge.

- **"Utilizing LLMs for Clinical Decision Support Systems"**  
  An analytical study on the effectiveness of LLMs in enhancing clinical decision-making processes through predictive analytics and patient history assessment.

- **"Ethical Considerations of AI in Health: Addressing the Challenges of LLM Usage"**  
  This paper delves into the ethical implications of deploying LLMs in healthcare settings, focusing on issues such as bias, accountability, and patient privacy.

- **"AI-Assisted Drug Discovery: The Role of LLMs"**  
  A discussion on how LLM-based agents are being utilized in the discovery and development of new pharmaceuticals, including drug repurposing strategies.

By exploring these papers, researchers and clinicians can gain insights into the evolving role of LLMs in advancing biology and medicine, ultimately improving patient outcomes and healthcare efficiency.
