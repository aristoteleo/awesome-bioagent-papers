# The Applications of LLM-based Agents in Biology and Medicine

Large Language Models (LLMs) have emerged as a significant technological advancement, providing innovative solutions in biology and medicine. Below is a list of papers that delve into various applications of LLMs in these fields, highlighting their transformative potential in medical research, clinical practice, and biological data analysis.

### Key Papers:

- **[LLM-based agents in biological knowledge extraction: A case study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1566253525000363)**  
  *Journal:* Artificial Intelligence in Medicine (2025)  
  *Summary:* This paper explores the applications of Language Model-based agents in the extraction of biological knowledge from various data sources. It presents a framework that utilizes large language models to facilitate the understanding and organization of complex biological data for medical research purposes.

- **[Lifelong multi-omics tracking reveals the role of immune function in health and disease](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-024-00717-2)**  
  *Journal:* Nature Medicine (2024-01-18)  
  *Summary:* This study discusses the integration of multi-omics data to analyze immune function over a lifetime, emphasizing its implications for health and disease. It presents findings that highlight the importance of immune system assessments in understanding individual variations in health outcomes, potentially guiding personalized medicine approaches.

- **[A survey of LLM applications in medical data analysis and decision making](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-024-10921-0)**  
  *Journal:* Artificial Intelligence (2024)  
  *Summary:* This paper surveys the various applications of Large Language Models (LLMs) in the field of medical data analysis and decision-making, showcasing their potential benefits and challenges in the biological and medical domains.

- **[Leveraging Large Language Models for Heuristic Search in Biology: A Case Study on RNA Folding](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06775v2)**  
  *Journal:* arXiv (2024)  
  *Summary:* This paper explores the use of LLMs for heuristic search problems in biological contexts, specifically examining RNA folding. It investigates how LLMs can enhance understanding and prediction of RNA secondary structures by utilizing their capacities in generating sequences and evaluating structural configurations.

- **[Leveraging Large Language Models in Biomedical Research: Application to Drug Repurposing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10180678/)**  
  *Journal:* Nature Communications (2023-10-01)  
  *Summary:* This paper discusses the application of LLMs in biomedical research, focusing on their role in drug repurposing. It outlines the potential for LLMs to analyze vast datasets, facilitate hypothesis generation, and improve information retrieval in medical contexts.

- **[Applications of deep learning in biology and medicine](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010482522002505)**  
  *Journal:* Medical Hypotheses (2022)  
  *Summary:* This paper reviews the applications of deep learning techniques across various fields of biology and medicine, focusing on how these methods are transforming research and healthcare by improving diagnosis, treatment personalization, and predictive modeling.

- **[Utilizing artificial intelligence to optimize clinical trial design and analysis](https://bmcmedresmethodol.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12874-019-0681-4)**  
  *Journal:* BMC Medical Research Methodology (2019)  
  *Summary:* This paper discusses the application of artificial intelligence, including language models, in the planning and conducting of clinical trials, highlighting how these technologies can improve efficiency and outcomes in medical research.

- **[Applications of LLMs in enhancing biomedicine: Opportunities and Challenges](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10286546)**  
  *Journal:* IEEE Access (2023)  
  *Summary:* This paper discusses the integration of LLMs into biomedicine, detailing various applications, potential advantages, and challenges encountered. It emphasizes the transformative impact of LLMs on areas such as drug discovery, personalized medicine, and clinical data analysis.
