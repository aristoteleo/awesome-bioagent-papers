# The Applications of LLM-Based Agents in Biology and Medicine

This document summarizes various important scholarly contributions that explore the role of large language models (LLMs) and artificial intelligence in the fields of biology and medicine. These papers cover a wide range of applications, from drug discovery to personalized medicine, demonstrating the transformative potential of LLMs in enhancing research methodologies, diagnostics, and patient care.

### List of Papers:

- **[LLM-Based Agents for Biomedical Purpose](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096)**  
  March 2025, arXiv  
  Discusses the applications of LLMs in biology and medicine, focusing on their abilities to assist in biomedical tasks, process data, and aid in patient care.

- **[Multimodal AI for Classification of Prostate Cancer from Biopsy Histopathology](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11583719/)**
