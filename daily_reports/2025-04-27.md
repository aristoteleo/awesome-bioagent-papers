# The Applications of LLM-based Agents in Biology and Medicine

This document provides an extensive overview of various papers discussing the integrative applications of large language model (LLM)-based agents across the fields of biology and medicine. These papers explore the capabilities of LLMs in enhancing research efficiency, supporting clinical decisions, and facilitating knowledge sharing among healthcare professionals.

### Listed Papers
- **[The applications of LLM-based agents in biology and medicine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096)** (arXiv, March 2025)
  - Discusses potential LLM applications in biology and medicine, including drug discovery and personalized medicine, as well as challenges faced.
  
- **[Harnessing large language models for drug discovery: current progress and future perspectives](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/ebiom/article/PIIS2352-3964(24)00482-1/fulltext)** (EBioMedicine, 2024)
  - Reviews applications of LLMs in drug discovery, highlighting advancements and future perspectives.
  
- **[Advancing the Frontiers of Biology with Large Language Models](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0092867424010705)** (Cell, 2024)
  - Discusses integrating LLMs in biological research applications, including genomics and drug discovery.
  
- **[Towards the Human-AI Collaboration: The Role of Large Language Models in Biology and Medicine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11211)** (arXiv, February 2025)
  - Explores how LLMs can enhance efficiency, clinical decisions, and complex biological understanding.
  
- **[Generative artificial intelligence in medicine and health: a systematic review](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-024-01083-y)** (Nature Medicine, 2024)
  - Evaluates applications of generative AI, focusing on LLMs, in medicine and health practices.
  
- **[Artificial intelligence in drug discovery and development: A review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2667102625000294)** (Drug Discovery Today, 2025)
  - Explores AI applications in drug discovery and development, with a spotlight on LLMs.
  
- **[Evaluating the performance of LLM-based agents in bioinformatics tasks](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-024-10921-0)** (Artificial Intelligence Review, 2024)
  - Highlights LLM-based agents in bioinformatics, exploring their strengths and limitations.
  
- **[Harnessing large language models for predicting protein structures](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-024-00717-2)** (Nature Reviews Bioengineering, 2024)
  - Discusses LLM applications in protein structure prediction and their implications in structural biology.
  
- **[Applications of large language models in drug discovery and development](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1566253525000363)** (Current Opinion in Pharmacology, 2025)
  - Focuses on LLM integration throughout drug discovery processes and their future outlook.
  
- **[Integrating Machine Learning with Biological Knowledge: A Case Study on Gene-Disease Associations](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44222-025-00279-5)** (Nature Reviews Genetics, 2025)
  - Highlights enhancing gene-disease association predictions through ML techniques.
  
- **[A deep learning approach for predicting protein-RNA interactions by combining structure and sequence information](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02354-y)** (Nature Methods, 2024)
  - Presents a framework for predicting protein-RNA interactions using deep learning methodologies.
  
- **[LLM-based Applications in Drug Discovery](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10802675/)** (Nature Reviews Drug Discovery, 2023)
   - Discusses LLM integration in drug discovery and enhancing research communication.
  
- **[Applications of LLM Agents in Biological Domains: A Comprehensive Review and Future Perspectives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06271)** (arXiv, 2025)
  - Reviews the current applications of LLMs in genomics and personalized medicine.
  
- **[Incorporating LLM-based Agents for Advanced Biological Data Analysis](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(24)01070-5)** (Cell, 2024)
  - Discusses LLM agents' contributions to analyzing complex biological datasets.
  
- **[The potential of large language models in pathology: A case for diagnostic support](https://www.modernpathology.org/article/S0893-3952(24)00268-0/fulltext)** (Modern Pathology, 2024)
  - Applications of LLMs in pathology for diagnostic support and efficiency in assessments.
  
- **[Deep learning and machine learning in medical image analysis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1566253518304482)** (Artificial Intelligence in Medicine, 2018)
  - Reviews deep learning and ML applications in medical image analysis, relevant for diagnostic advancements.
  
- **[Exploring the Potential of Large Language Models for Biological Data Interpretation](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13721-024-00458-1)** (Biochemistry and Biophysics Reports, 2024)
  - Reviews LLM capabilities in interpreting biological data across various fields.
  
- **[Language models as algorithms for biological interpretation](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg861)** (Nature Reviews Genetics, 2023)
  - Discusses how language models enhance understanding and streamline biological processes.
  
- **[Deep Learning in Drug Discovery and Development: Opportunities and Challenges](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.jcim.4c01907)** (Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling, 2021)
  - Discusses DEEP learning technologies' impact and challenges in drug development.
  
- **[Transforming Type 2 Diabetes Treatment Using ChatGPT: A Case Series](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(24)01070-5)** (Cell, 2024)
  - Discusses ChatGPT applications in personalized Type 2 Diabetes management.
  
- **[The Applications of Machine Learning in Biology and Medicine: A Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11839394/)** (Nature Reviews Genetics, 2023)
  - Highlights machine learning advancements in biological applications, including LLM potential.
  
- **[LLM-based Agents for Large Scale Cancer Research: Automating Literature Review and Data Extraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14656)** (ArXiv, January 2024)
  - Discusses LLM agents for improving literature review and data extraction in cancer research.
  
- **[ChatGPT-based Interventions for Improving Health Literacy: A Pilot Study](https://bmcmedinformdecismak.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12911-025-02954-4)** (BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making, 2025)
  - Explores ChatGPT's effectiveness in improving patient health literacy.
  
- **[The Role of Language Models in Biomedical Research: Status and Directions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11172607/)** (Nature Reviews Drug Discovery, 2023)
  - Discusses language models' roles in biomedical research areas.

- **[Applying Large Language Models to Conduct Qualitative Interview Studies: A Feasibility Study](https://journals.plos.org/digitalhealth/article?id=10.1371/journal.pdig.0000662)** (PLOS Digital Health, 2023)
  - Examines the feasibility of using LLMs in qualitative health research.  

These papers collectively emphasize the transformative potential of LLMs in enhancing various facets of biological research and medical practice, paving the way for innovative solutions in these critical fields.
