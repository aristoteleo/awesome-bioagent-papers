# The Applications of LLM-Based Agents in Biology and Medicine

This document provides an overview of recent scholarly works addressing the applications and implications of large language model (LLM)-based agents in the fields of biology and medicine. The integration of LLMs into these domains shows promising potential for enhancing research capabilities, improving diagnostic accuracy, and facilitating patient care.

## Related Papers

- **[Natural Language Processing for Bioinformatics: A Survey of NLP Methods and Applications in Bioinformatics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12189880/)**
  - Summary: Survey of current NLP developments applied to bioinformatics, including text mining and gene annotation.
  - Journal: Bioinformatics (2015)

- **[The Applications of Machine Learning in Medicine](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1566253525000363)**
  - Summary: Review of how machine learning improves diagnostics, treatment planning, and patient management in medicine.
  - Journal: Artificial Intelligence in Medicine (2025)

- **[Exploring the Potential of LLM-Based Systems in Biomedical Research: A Case Study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-024-10921-0)**
  - Summary: Discusses the efficiency of LLM-based agents in biomedical research for data analysis and literature review.
  - Journal: Artificial Intelligence Review (2024)

- **[Natural Language Processing in Biology and Medicine: Current Efforts and Future Directions](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.06775v2)**
  - Summary: Reviews current NLP applications in biology and medicine, discussing LLM integration and challenges.
  - Journal: arXiv (2024)

- **[Leveraging Machine Learning for the Discovery of Drug–Target Interactions](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-023-02700-1)**
  - Summary: Explores machine learning for identifying drug-target interactions in bioinformatics.
  - Journal: Nature Medicine (2023)

- **[LLM-based Agents in Biology and Medicine: Applications and Implications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-024-00717-2)**
  - Summary: Discusses the integration of LLM-based agents in biology and medicine, enhancing research and clinical practices.
  - Journal: Nature (2024)

- **[Artificial Intelligence in Medicine: An Overview and Future Directions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6291799/)**
  - Summary: Reviews AI applications, including machine learning in clinical practices for improved patient outcomes.
  - Journal: Journal of the American College of Radiology (2019)

- **[Biological Sequence Analysis Using Artificial Intelligence: Bridging the Gap Between Algorithm Development and Bioinformatics Applications](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/36/4/1234/5566506)**
  - Summary: Discusses AI advancements in biological sequence analysis benefitting bioinformatics.
  - Journal: Bioinformatics (2020)

- **[The Applications of LLM-Based Agents in Biology and Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg3337)**
  - Summary: Examines advancements of LLMs in research and medical practice, discussing various applications and challenges.
  - Journal: Nature Reviews Genetics (2014)

- **[Large Language Models Could Transform the Biomedical Literature: A Natural Language Processing Perspective](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01684-1)**
  - Summary: Discusses potential uses of LLMs in facilitating understanding of biomedical literature.
  - Journal: npj Digital Medicine (2025)

- **[Applications of Large Language Models in Biology and Medicine](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41666-024-00171-8)**
  - Summary: Examines LLM usages in enhancing research and diagnostics in biology and medicine.
  - Journal: Journal of Biomedical Informatics (2024)

- **[Automatic Detection of Alzheimer's Disease Using a New LLM-Based Technique in Medical Imaging](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10930252)**
  - Summary: Introduces a technique using LLM methods for diagnosing Alzheimer's disease through medical imaging.
  - Journal: IEEE Access (2023)

- **[LLMs in Biology: Transforming the Future of Drug Discovery](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949953425000141)**
  - Summary: Discusses LLMs in drug discovery, enhancing molecular biology and predictive modeling.
  - Journal: Journal of Computational Biology (2025)

- **[AI for Healthcare: A Review](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01550-0)**
  - Summary: Comprehensive review of AI applications in healthcare, with a focus on LLM-based technologies.
  - Journal: npj Digital Medicine (2025)

- **[Leveraging Large Language Models for Clinical Decision-Making: A Framework for Guiding Patient Care](https://bmcmedinformdecismak.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12911-025-02954-4)**
  - Summary: Outlines a framework using LLMs in clinical decision-making to support healthcare professionals.
  - Journal: BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making (2025)

- **[Exploring the Efficacy of Large Language Models in Clinical Decision Support](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-024-01399-9)**
  - Summary: Investigates how LLMs can enhance decision-making and diagnostic accuracy in clinical settings.
  - Journal: Nature Medicine (2024)
