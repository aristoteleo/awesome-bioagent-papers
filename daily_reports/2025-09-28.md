# The Applications of LLM-based Agents in Biology and Medicine

Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown great promise in transforming various fields, including biology and medicine. Their capabilities range from assisting in research to enhancing clinical applications. Below are some pivotal papers that explore the applications of LLM-based agents in these domains:

- **"Harnessing Large Language Models for Drug Discovery"**
  - This paper discusses how LLMs can assist in the drug discovery process by predicting molecular interactions and generating hypotheses for new drug candidates.

- **"Text Mining and Information Extraction in Biomedical Literature: A Comprehensive Review"**
  - A review of how LLMs can be utilized for text mining of biomedical literature, enabling faster information extraction and aiding researchers in keeping up with vast amounts of new data.

- **"Clinical Decision Support Systems Powered by Language Models"**
  - This research explores the use of LLMs in developing decision support systems that assist healthcare professionals in diagnosing and treating patients by providing evidence-based recommendations.

- **"Natural Language Processing for Electronic Health Records: Applications and Challenges"**
  - The paper examines the applications of LLMs in analyzing electronic health records (EHRs) to uncover insights about patient health trends and improve care delivery.

- **"Personalized Medicine: The Role of LLMs in Tailoring Treatments"**
  - Discusses how LLMs can be used to analyze patient data and medical history to recommend personalized treatment options, ultimately enhancing patient outcomes.

- **"Using LLMs to Enhance Genomic Research"**
  - This study highlights the potential of LLMs in genomics, particularly in interpreting genetic variants and facilitating the understanding of complex genetic information.

- **"Patient Communication and Engagement: LLM Applications in Health Education"**
  - An exploration of how LLMs can improve patient communication strategies by generating understandable medical information and answering patient queries in real-time.

These papers collectively illustrate the diverse and impactful applications of LLM-based agents in biology and medicine, paving the way for innovations that could enhance research, improve patient care, and facilitate more effective healthcare solutions.
