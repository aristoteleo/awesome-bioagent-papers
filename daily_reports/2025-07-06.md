# The Applications of LLM-Based Agents in Biology and Medicine

This markdown document summarizes recent research examining the various applications of large language model (LLM)-based agents within the fields of biology and medicine. The studies illustrate how these advanced AI technologies are transforming research, diagnostics, and therapeutic practices.

## Key Papers

- **[The role of Language Models in Biomedical Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096)**  
  Discusses the utilization of LLMs in biomedical research, highlighting applications such as data annotation and patient care.  
  *Published: March 2025* - *Journal: arXiv*

- **[A novel LLM-based framework for drug discovery and personalized medicine](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.03.11.642548v1)**  
  Presents a framework for drug discovery utilizing LLMs to generate hypotheses and analyze biological data.  
  *Published: 2025* - *Journal: bioRxiv*

- **[Transforming Common Practice In Therapeutics with Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing Technologies](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8432320/)**  
  Discusses the impact of AI and NLP technologies on therapeutic practices to improve patient care.  
  *Published: 2021* - *Journal: Frontiers in Pharmacology*

- **[Leveraging large language models for systematic review in genetics research: a case study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s10038-024-01231-y)**  
  Explores the application of LLMs in enhancing the systematic review process in genetics.  
  *Published: 2024* - *Journal: Journal of Human Genetics*

- **[LLM-based BERT-Well: A Novel Framework for Biomedical Text Mining](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-024-10921-0)**  
  Discusses LLM-based BERT-Well for enhancing biomedical text mining tasks.  
  *Published: 2024* - *Journal: Artificial Intelligence Review*

- **[Lifelong Learning Unveils the Cost of Meaningful Interpretability in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05694)**  
  Examines the challenges of interpretability in LLMs concerning their application in healthcare.  
  *Published: October 2023* - *Journal: arXiv*

- **[Application of artificial intelligence to improve medication adherence](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/hcs2.61)**  
  Integrates AI technologies to enhance medication adherence among patients.  
  *Published: 2021* - *Journal: Health Services Research*

- **[Artificial intelligence in biomedicine: A roadmap for the future](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-024-00944-1)**  
  Outlines potential AI applications in genomics and personalized medicine.  
  *Published: 2024* - *Journal: Nature Machine Intelligence*

- **[AI and machine learning in COVID-19 research: the Lancet Digital Health series](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(25)00202-8/fulltext)**  
  Discusses the impact of AI in enhancing COVID-19 research and treatment planning.  
  *Published: 2023* - *Journal: The Lancet*

- **[Applications of Large Language Models in Healthcare: A Systematic Review](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-024-01258-7)**  
  Analyzes LLM applications in clinical decision support and patient communication.  
  *Published: 2024* - *Journal: Nature Medicine*

- **[Investigating the Role of AI-Driven Large Language Models in Drug Discovery](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10920625/)**  
  Discusses LLM applications in drug discovery processes.  
  *Published: July 2023* - *Journal: Nature Reviews Drug Discovery*

- **[Leveraging Large Language Models for Mental Health: Opportunities and Challenges](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/hcs2.61)**  
  Addresses how LLMs can enhance therapeutic communication in mental health.  
  *Published: 2023* - *Journal: Health Services Research*

- **[A Survey on Large Language Models in the Life Sciences and Healthcare](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.16860v1)**  
  Explores the landscape of LLM applications in life sciences.  
  *Published: September 2024* - *Journal: ArXiv*

- **[Large Language Models Encode Biological Knowledge](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03520-1)**  
  Examines how LLMs encode biological knowledge for diagnostics and therapeutic development.  
  *Published: 2025* - *Journal: Nature Medicine*

- **[Harnessing the power of large language models for state-of-the-art genomic medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-024-02534-3)**  
  Discusses LLMs in genomic medicine to enhance data interpretation and patient outcomes.  
  *Published: 2024* - *Journal: Nature Biotechnology*

- **[Exploring the Applications of LLM-Based Agents in Biology and Medicine](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11339493/)**  
  Reviews the role of LLMs in diagnostics and research.  
  *Published: 2023* - *Journal: Journal of Biomedical Informatics*

- **[Applications of Large Language Models in Biology and Medicine](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-biodatasci-102224-074736)**  
  Reviews applications of LLMs in analyzing and interpreting biological data.  
  *Published: 2023* - *Journal: Annual Review of Bioinformatics and Data Science*

This compilation provides an insightful glance into evolving methodologies leveraging LLMs in biology and medicine, showcasing a fundamental shift toward integrating AI in health-related processes.
