# The Applications of LLM-Based Agents in Biology and Medicine

The integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) into biology and medicine has opened new avenues for research, diagnostics, and clinical decision-making. This collection of papers highlights various applications, methodologies, and future directions for utilizing LLM-based agents in these fields.

## Relevant Papers

- **[Applications of Artificial Intelligence in Health Care: Review and Future Directions](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010482522002505)**  
  *Journal: Surgery (2022)*  
  This paper reviews the applications of artificial intelligence (AI) in healthcare, focusing on the integration of machine learning and deep learning in clinical practices. It discusses various methodologies, effectiveness in different medical domains, and outlines future directions for AI-driven solutions.

- **[Applications of Deep Learning and Artificial Intelligence in Medical Diagnosis: An Overview](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/9/22/2970)**  
  *Journal: Mathematics (2021)*  
  This paper provides an overview of the applications of deep learning and AI in medical diagnosis, discussing models and implications for future practices in processing biological data.

- **[Enhancing the Efficacy of Deep Learning-Based Microbiome Analysis via Transfer Learning with Unsupervised Pre-Training](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10968491)**  
  *Journal: IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering (2023)*  
  This study presents a novel approach to improve deep learning in microbiome analysis through transfer learning and unsupervised pre-training, emphasizing the importance of microbiome research in biology and medicine.

- **[Exploring the Potential of LLMs in Biology and Medicine: A Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11984503/)**  
  *Journal: Journal of Biomedical Informatics (2023)*  
  This review discusses the application of LLMs in biology and medicine, focusing on data analysis, hypothesis generation, and insights into biological processes.

- **[Predicting Protein-Protein Interactions from Sequence Data with Deep Learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-56989-2)**  
  *Journal: Nature Communications (2025)*  
  This paper presents a deep learning approach to predict protein-protein interactions, demonstrating effective applications of LLMs in biological research.

- **[The Role of LLM-Based Agents in Biological Research and Medicine: Opportunities and Challenges](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41666-024-00171-8)**  
  *Journal: Biomedical Artificial Intelligence (2024)*  
  This paper discusses the applications of LLM-based agents in biology and medicine, identifying opportunities for research assistance and challenges in ethical implementation.

- **[Harnessing Large Language Models to Revisit Antibody Repertoires](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mef2.70019?msockid=2cd5c2b3c6cc6c2305a4d4ddc73b6d8d)**  
  *Journal: Molecular Ecology (2023)*  
  This study explores how LLMs can enhance the analysis of antibody repertoires, providing insights into immune diversity and vaccine development.

- **[Evaluating the Accuracy of ChatGPT in Dermatology: A Cross-Sectional Study of 18 Common Dermatologic Diagnoses](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11339493/)**  
  *Journal: Journal of Investigative Dermatology (2023)*  
  This study evaluates the performance of ChatGPT in dermatology by comparing its diagnostic accuracy against expert dermatologists.

These papers collectively illustrate the transformative impact of LLMs in the fields of biology and medicine, as well as the ongoing challenges and future potential in these critical areas.
